Do I need an account?

Yes, a free Academy account, so your progress and certificate are saved. It's separate from your OpenMRS ID.

Can I run a course for my team?

Course material is open. Many implementers run Fundamentals as a classroom day; ask on Talk for facilitator notes.

Can I contribute a course?

Yes. The Secure Coding Course came from the community. Propose one on Talk in the Academy category.
