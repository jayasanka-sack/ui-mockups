OpenMRS 3: a modern EMR on a proven platform.

Patient chart, forms, queues, appointments, lab and pharmacy workflows. Offline-tolerant and translatable. Runs on a laptop, a server or a national cloud.

FRONTEND

O3 apps

Modular frontend apps for registration, charting, orders, labs and more.

CLINICAL CONTENT

CIEL dictionary

STANDARDS

In the field · rotates quarterly

Kenya's national EMR moved to OpenMRS 3 one workflow at a time.

KenyaEMR runs in more than 2,000 facilities. Rather than switch everything at once, the team started with the workflows clinics felt most: queues and appointments. In a two-week hackathon they converted 60 forms to the O3 form engine, then rolled the new chart out alongside the old one until sites were ready to move.
