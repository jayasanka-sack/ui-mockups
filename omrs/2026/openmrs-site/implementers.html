For implementers

From one clinic to a national programme, the same platform.

Implementing OpenMRS means standing up a server, loading your forms and concepts, training the people who will use it, and keeping it running. This page says what that involves at each scale, what you'll need, and where to get help. Nothing here requires you to write code.

One site, a few dozen users

Run the Reference Application or a distribution on one server in the facility, or hosted. Start with the standard forms and CIEL concepts, add your own as you go.

One IT lead, one clinical champion

Hundreds to thousands of facilities

What you'll need

Five things every deployment has.

The software is the easy part. Plan for these and the rest follows.

  1. 01

    People

    A clinical lead who owns the workflows, someone who can run a server, and super-users at each site.

  2. 02

    Infrastructure

    A server (4 cores, 8 GB, 100 GB SSD minimum), power, a local network, and a browser on each workstation. Internet helps but isn't required day to day.

  3. 03

    Content

    Your forms, concepts, locations, programmes and reports. Start from CIEL and the standard forms; version your own as a content package.

  4. 04

    Training

    OpenMRS Fundamentals on the Academy is free and takes a day. Plan hands-on sessions at each site before go-live.

  5. 05

    Support and upgrades

    Who answers the phone, who takes backups, who tests the next release. In-house, a partner, or both.

How to extend without forking

Make it yours. Keep the upgrades.

Most of what makes a deployment local lives outside the core: configuration, content, and modules you own. Your own modules can stay private. Changes to OpenMRS code you distribute go back to everyone.

NO CODE

Configure

Locations, identifiers, visit types, roles, languages, branding.

NO CODE

Build forms

Clinical forms in the Form Builder, published without a deploy.

CONTENT

Manage concepts

CIEL or your own dictionary, through Open Concept Lab.

CODE

Modules and apps

Integrations and workflows as plug-ins, yours to keep or share.
